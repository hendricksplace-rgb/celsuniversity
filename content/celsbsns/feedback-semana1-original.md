## English

📈 Feedback & Progress – Week 1

Title: Feedback & Progress
Estimated Time: 1 Hour
Goal: Reflect on your own performance and identify where you need support or improvement.

🧭 Instructions:

This is your chance to pause, review your work, and measure your own progress. Use this time to reflect on your learning, evaluate how confident you feel, and request help if needed.

📝 Step 1: Self-Evaluation Checklist

Read each statement below and mark yourself: ✅ Yes / 🤔 Maybe / ❌ Not Yet

I submitted my written introduction using a professional tone

I recorded and uploaded my Capstone video introduction

I used at least 10 vocabulary words from the dictionary this week

I practiced my spoken English out loud in all 3 speaking exercises

I reviewed the examples in the reading and applied them in my work

I am speaking more clearly and with more confidence than before

🧭 Step 2: Short Reflection

Answer these questions:

What was the hardest part of this week's tasks?

Where do you feel more confident now?

What specific area would you like more help with?

🧭 Step 3: Request Feedback (Optional)

If you would like additional feedback or support from your CelsUniversity team:

Check the box below.

A member of our team will review your work and contact you via email or platform message.

☐ Yes, I'd like a coach to check my Capstone video
☐ Yes, I'd like help improving my written communication
☐ Yes, I need help understanding the expectations better

🧭 Step 4: AI Practice & Feedback (Optional but Recommended)

Use ChatGPT with voice mode to practice your spoken English and get instant feedback. This is a powerful tool to build your confidence and improve clarity.

Here's how to do it:

Open ChatGPT and switch to voice mode (available on mobile).

Tap the microphone and say your 30-second introduction or pitch.

Example: "Hi, my name is Mariana. I'm an industrial engineer with five years of experience because I want to grow my career and contribute to global projects…"

See how ChatGPT responds:

Did it understand your message?

Did it ask you to repeat anything?

What did it think you were saying?

Then ask ChatGPT:

Can you give me feedback on my pronunciation and clarity?

Was there anything that was hard to understand?

How could I improve how I speak?

You can also say:

Let's practice. I'll speak, and you give me feedback on my tone, grammar, and vocabulary.

Goal: If ChatGPT understands you clearly on the first try—you're probably speaking clearly! If not, review your words, slow down, and try again. This is a great private way to practice before doing your final Capstone video.

🧭 Paso 4: Práctica y Retroalimentación con IA (Opcional pero Recomendado)

Aquí te explicamos cómo hacerlo:

Abre ChatGPT y cambia al modo de voz (disponible en móvil).

Toca el micrófono y di tu introducción o presentación de 30 segundos.

Ejemplo: "Hi, my name is Mariana. I'm an industrial engineer with five years of experience because I want to grow my career and contribute to global projects…"

Observa cómo responde ChatGPT:

¿Entendió tu mensaje?

¿Te pidió que repitieras algo?

¿Qué pensó que estabas diciendo?

Luego pregunta a ChatGPT:

Can you give me feedback on my pronunciation and clarity?

Was there anything that was hard to understand?

How could I improve how I speak?

También puedes decir:

Let's practice. I'll speak, and you give me feedback on my tone, grammar, and vocabulary.

Objetivo: Si ChatGPT te entiende claramente en el primer intento, ¡probablemente estás hablando claramente! Si no, revisa tus palabras, disminuye la velocidad y vuelve a intentarlo. Esta es una excelente manera privada de practicar antes de hacer tu video final del Proyecto Final.
