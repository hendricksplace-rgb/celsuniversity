## English

📹 Video Submission – Week 1

Title: Introduce Yourself Like a Pro
Estimated Time: 1 Hour
Goal: Record and submit a short professional video introduction demonstrating clarity, tone, and confidence.

🎥 Assignment Overview:

You've studied what clear, confident, and professional communication looks like—and now it's your turn.

In this task, you'll record a 2-minute video introducing yourself as if you were speaking to a future employer or team. This video will be part of your Capstone Project and will serve as your first speaking evaluation.

🧭 Instructions:
Use your written self-introduction (from the writing task) as your base.

Record yourself saying it naturally, using:

A clear and calm tone

Professional body language

Natural pacing and pronunciation

Tips for a strong video:

📱 Use your phone's camera (horizontal is best)

🎤 Check your mic—record in a quiet space

💡 Face a light source so your face is clearly visible

👔 Wear something neat (doesn't need to be formal, just clean and presentable)

👁 Make eye contact with the camera, like you're talking to a real person

😌 Smile once or twice—it shows confidence and friendliness

Length:
Aim for 1:30 to 2:00 minutes. It's okay if you go a little over if the quality is strong.

✅ Your video should include:
A confident introduction with your name and role

A short summary of your professional background

Your goals or motivation for joining a U.S. company

A personal detail to show who you are outside of work

📝 Submission:
Upload your video (MP4 or MOV preferred) to the course platform. If you're using your phone, transfer it via email, upload link, or directly from the CelsU mobile interface.

REGISTER and USE Google Classroom: https://classroom.google.com/c/NzgzNjY5MDgwMDkx?cjc=rllut6hy

You'll receive feedback on:

Clarity

Tone

Body language

Overall presentation

This is your first step in becoming a confident communicator in English—and it sets the tone for everything that comes next.
