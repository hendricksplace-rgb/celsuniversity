## English

✍️ Writing Task – Week 1

Title: Professional Self-Introduction Email
Estimated Time: 1–2 Hours
Goal: Practice clear, confident, and professional written communication while preparing your Capstone introduction content.

📄 Assignment Overview:

This week, you'll write a formal self-introduction email as if you were joining a new company in the United States. This email will demonstrate your ability to write professionally, apply clarity and tone, and follow the expected format in U.S. business settings.

🧭 Instructions:
Imagine this scenario:
You've just joined a company in the U.S. as part of your new job. Your supervisor has asked you to introduce yourself to your department by email.

Your email should include:

A greeting (use a professional tone)

Your name and role

A short summary of your background

What you look forward to in this role

One personal detail (hobbies, interests)

A polite closing

Word Count:
150–250 words

Tone & Structure:

Clear and respectful

Positive and professional

Use paragraph spacing and standard email format (no bullet points or emojis)

✅ Example (Template for Guidance):

Subject: Introduction – Matt Hendricks

Dear Team,

My name is Matt Hendricks, and I've just joined the company as a Training and Development Specialist. I've worked in operations and executive leadership for over 15 years, and I'm passionate about building strong teams that communicate clearly and deliver results.

I'm excited to be part of a company that values growth, and I look forward to learning from all of you while contributing to our shared goals. Outside of work, I enjoy soccer, salsa music, and community volunteer work.

Please feel free to reach out—I'd love to connect!

Best regards,
Matt Hendricks
