## English (body)

📖 Reading Assignment – Week 1

Title: "Clear, Confident, and Professional: How U.S. Business Communication Really Works"
Estimated Time: 1 Hour
Type: Case Study + Reflection Questions

Summary:
This reading introduces learners to the expectations of communication in U.S. professional settings. It focuses on the importance of clarity, tone, and confidence, using three short case studies based on real workplace interactions.

📄 Content:

📚 Article: Forbes – Why Communication Is Today's Most Important Skill
🎥 Video: TEDx – The Power of Effective Communication — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v3XJ5mC2hWQ

Case Study 1: The Confusing Email
Maria, a new hire at a logistics company in Texas, sends the following message:

"Hi, I was just wondering if maybe you could kind of help me understand this client's request. I think I get it, but I'm not totally sure…"

Her supervisor is confused. He doesn't respond for three days.

🔍 Reflection Questions:

What would you change in Maria's email to make it clearer and more professional?

How might this delay affect her performance or reputation?

Case Study 2: The Right Tone Gets Results
Daniel needs to ask a coworker to turn in a late report. He writes:

"Hey, I noticed the report wasn't submitted. Would it be possible to have it by 2 PM today? Let me know if you need anything from me."

The coworker replies immediately and delivers on time.

🔍 Reflection Questions:

Why was Daniel's message effective?

How did his tone influence the outcome?

Case Study 3: Confidence in a Difficult Moment
A team is late on a project. One person says:

"This wasn't my fault. I was waiting for others."

Another says:

"We've had some setbacks. I've updated the plan and will meet with the team this afternoon to get us back on track."

🔍 Reflection Questions:

Who sounds more professional? Why?

What does the second person demonstrate about leadership?

📝 Instructions:
Read all three case studies and answer the reflection questions in a separate document or journal. You'll refer to your responses later during your writing and speaking tasks.

Estudio de Caso 1: El Correo Electrónico Confuso
María, una nueva empleada en una empresa de logística en Texas, envía el siguiente mensaje:

"Hi, I was just wondering if maybe you could kind of help me understand this client's request. I think I get it, but I'm not totally sure…"

🔍 Preguntas de Reflexión:

Estudio de Caso 2: El Tono Correcto Consigue Resultados
Daniel necesita pedirle a un compañero de trabajo que entregue un informe atrasado. Escribe:

"Hey, I noticed the report wasn't submitted. Would it be possible to have it by 2 PM today? Let me know if you need anything from me."

El compañero responde de inmediato y entrega a tiempo.

🔍 Preguntas de Reflexión:

¿Por qué fue efectivo el mensaje de Daniel?
