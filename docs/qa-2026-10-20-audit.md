# CelsU audit follow-ups: logged-in test pass (C3)

Branch `audit-fixes` (base main c1a3107, then main 0ce614d merged in). Tested against a local production build (`next build` + `next start -p 3700`) with two throwaway full-access students (enrollments in all 7 published courses plus the International Professional Development program), one per viewport. Tools: Playwright (chromium-1194) at 360×780 and 1440×900.

Each page was checked for HTTP status, console errors, failed requests, horizontal overflow (scrollWidth > innerWidth), blank sections and near-empty bodies. Requests to Vimeo, YouTube, Wistia and TED can't be reached from this sandbox (ERR_TUNNEL_CONNECTION_FAILED), so they were ignored.

Screenshots of failures: `audit-shots/`. The final run produced no page failures. The interaction "fails" listed under *Test artifacts* come from re-running the suite on the same users, and each one was checked in the DB.

## Broken

### Fixed in this branch
1. **Empty videos counted toward progress and could be "finished" in seconds.** A video block with no file counted as "seen" after 2 s. The CelsVideo part showed a Finish button under an empty player. The lesson page recorded and finished lessons that had nothing in them.
   - Now: courseStats (Inicio, Mis cursos, Progreso, Mis clases, CelsGLOBAL route) counts only lessons with content.
   - The CelsTV path keeps an empty CelsVideo as "Próximamente" but leaves it out of x/N, the episode state and resume.
   - The CelsBSNS week rows, route map and current week ignore empty videos.
   - The video part and lesson page show "Video próximamente" and don't record or finish it.
   - Vimeo and file videos are now tracked to 90 % / end, instead of counting as done after 2 s on screen.
2. **CelsUS Week 2 printable PDFs returned 400.** The cause was not a missing login. The page signed the URLs when it loaded, with a 10-minute token: signed 01:12:12, expired 01:22:12, tapped 01:39–01:40.
   - New `/app/archivo?ref=…` signs on the server at tap time, with the viewer's session and course access, then redirects.
   - Used for the printable PDFs, lesson PDF/file Open/Download buttons, and the Capstone resume "Ver mi PDF". That one used to sign in the browser and then call `window.open` after an await, which Safari blocks.
3. **Inicio's "new lesson" band could name an old guided lesson.** It now skips guided modules.
4. **Wrong totals:**
   - Mis cursos showed "Semana 1 de 11" (CelsBSNS) and "Módulo 1 de 7" (CelsUS) because Recursos adicionales, Material extra and Evaluación were counted. Now 10 and 5. The CelsGLOBAL route uses the same count.
5. **Lesson page for a video lesson with no blocks at all** showed "Esta lección todavía no tiene contenido" with a Finish button. It now shows "Video próximamente" and records nothing.

### Content for Cowork
- 37 of 62 CelsBSNS video lessons have no video ("Video pending — add the lesson video here.", or no block at all, e.g. "Week 3 Video 5"). They are hidden from students now. Weeks 7–10 have no videos visible at all.
- CelsTV: 37 CelsVideo lessons have no video (episodes 3+). They show "Video próximamente".
- CelsBSNS titles are inconsistent: week 1 module is "Module 1: …" while others are "Week N – …". Week 2 videos 04 and 05 are both "Difficult Conversations". "Week 02:06" differs from the "Week 2: 0N" pattern. Week 4 shows Video 01/03/05/06/07 because 02 and 04 are empty.
- Guided courses have old lessons with has_content=false (e.g. CV step 7 Video/YouTube/Downloads/Activity, Interview step 7 Video/YouTube/Downloads). Students never see them; listed only for cleanup.
- The CelsUS course page has no link labelled "Examen final". The exams live at /app/examen-civico, linked from the course header, and in the Evaluación module.

### Needs Matt
- Server-side guard: `record_lesson_progress` still accepts a finish for a lesson with has_content=false. The UI no longer calls it, but an old cached client or an offline-queued finish could still complete one. Suggest refusing `p_finish` when has_content is false: a small migration, not done here.
- Other page-load signed URLs still expire after 10 minutes on a page left open: audio in guided steps, CelsTV parts, CelsBooks. Tarjetas already has a refresh action. Same root cause as the PDFs; the fix would be a refresh-on-error for audio players.
- CelsTV ep 1–2 videos: completion now needs 90 % watched (Vimeo player events) or the button. Before, the part was marked done 2 s after opening.

### Test artifacts (not bugs)
Speaking regex, Tarjetas track already chosen, and guided check answers already locked on re-runs: see the notes above. Each one was checked directly in the DB.
